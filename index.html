DPS Tips

1Lower your general sensitivity by 5–10 each match until headshots feel natural.
2Turn OFF "Fire Button Auto-Aim" if you want full drag control.
3Practice in Training Ground for 10 min before ranked.

Headshot Tips

1Aim slightly above the shoulder line, then drag down as you fire.
2Use 2-finger claw for faster drag control.
3M1887 + MP40 = deadliest close-range combo.
4Always keep crosshair at head level while moving.

Rank Push Tips

1Land at edge locations early game to survive longer.
2Play with a squad — solo rank pushes are much harder.
3Avoid hot drops unless you're confident in your aim.
4Use gloo walls aggressively — don't save them.
